About

I'm Om Kumbhar. I taught myself to build things because I wanted them to exist, not because a syllabus asked for it. Most of what I know came from shipping something badly and then having to fix it in front of people who were using it.

Trovro started from a simple observation: the people doing the actual work in this city hand over a cut of it to whoever introduced them to the customer. So I built something that doesn't take the cut, and I've spent the last stretch getting it through design, a full backend migration, three languages, and into closed testing on Google Play.

Alongside that I take freelance web clients, keep a few Android projects running, and I'm working through the internals of language models a line at a time. Long term I want to run a company. Trovro is the first serious attempt.
